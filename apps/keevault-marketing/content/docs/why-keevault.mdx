title: "Why Keevault"

---

The [Zeabur environment-variable incident](https://status.zeabur.com/incident/1037896)
in August 2026 prompted this project. Zeabur confirmed credential exposure;
[ABMedia's coverage on Binance Square](https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/361221256902006)
reported unauthorized AI-service charges.

I wanted to store app secrets separately from the deployment platform's saved
variables and approve each process before it receives them. Keevault adds that
approval step. It cannot protect secrets from someone controlling an already
approved app or its host. See [security basics](/docs/security).

## Why Cloudflare

Keevault is primarily designed to be hosted on Cloudflare. I chose it because
I trust its security controls more for storing my app secrets. Its published
[security policies and independent certifications](https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/)
gave me more confidence in that choice.

Your applications can still run on Zeabur, a VPS, or another container platform.
Cloudflare hosts the vault; your app requests its secrets when it starts.
