title: "Developer guide"
description: "Connect your application to Keevault and launch it with approved secrets."
---

Keevault supplies secrets to your application as environment variables. The
client waits for approval before starting your command, so you can keep secret
values out of source code, config files, and container images.

You'll need a vault URL, an environment with your application's secrets, a
bootstrap token, and the keevault client binary. Approving a start requires an
admin or owner account; a teammate can approve it for you.

## Integrate your application

[Getting started](/docs/getting-started) walks through configuring
`keevault.json`, running the client, and reading secrets in your application.

For a container deployment, follow the [Docker example](/docs/examples/docker).

## Configure and maintain the integration

- [Client configuration](/docs/client) covers JSON launch settings and exit codes.
- [Managing secrets](/docs/managing-secrets) covers environment values and bootstrap tokens.
- [Approving boots](/docs/approving-boots) explains what happens at each start or redeployment.
- [Security basics](/docs/security) explains the limits of approval and what your application must protect.
